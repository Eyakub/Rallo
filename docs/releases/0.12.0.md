## What's new

- **Hold Right ⌥ to talk.** Voice typing no longer needs a four-key shortcut: hold **Right ⌥**,
  speak, and let go; Rallo types what you said into the app you're in. For longer thoughts,
  **double-tap Right ⌥** and Rallo keeps listening until you press it again. ⌃⌥⌘V still
  starts and stops it too. Prefer another key? Settings → Voice offers **Right ⌘**, and each
  way can be turned off. Rallo watches only the modifier keys, never what you type.
  Hands-free listening stops by itself after 2 minutes of silence.
- **Pick the Whisper model that suits you.** Settings → Voice → Model:

  | Model | Download | Languages |
  |---|---|---|
  | Large-v3 turbo, 16-bit | 1.6 GB | about 99, including Bangla |
  | Large-v3 turbo, 8-bit | 874 MB | the same, with nearly the same accuracy |
  | Small | 190 MB | English only, and fast |

  Already have the 16-bit model? It stays selected; nothing downloads again.
- **No more phantom text.** Whisper sometimes "hears" a stock phrase like "Thank you." in
  background noise. Rallo now drops those, and doesn't transcribe or send long stretches of
  steady noise (a fan, an air conditioner), with Whisper and with the cloud engine.

## Languages, by engine

- **Apple** (macOS 26): your Mac's language, if it's one of the 33 Apple supports
  (54 regional variants). Bangla isn't one of them.
- **Whisper Large-v3 turbo**: about 99 languages, including Bangla. Set Language to
  Automatic, English or Bangla.
- **Whisper Small**: English only.
- **Cloud**: whatever your provider supports; Whisper-based ones (Groq, OpenAI) cover about 99.

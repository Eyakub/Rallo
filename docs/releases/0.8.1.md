## Fixes

- **Fewer Keychain prompts.** Rallo reads your ClickUp token and cloud voice key at most once
  per launch, and Settings → Voice no longer reads the key just to show that one is saved.
- Why macOS still asks once after each update: Rallo is signed with its own certificate, not
  an Apple Developer ID, so macOS remembers "Always Allow" per build. Click **Always Allow**
  (with your login password) and it won't ask again until the next update.

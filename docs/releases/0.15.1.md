## What's new

- **Formatting marks never show.** In the Notes window, `**`, `*`, `~~`, backticks, `# `, `> `
  and code-block fences stay hidden, even on the line you're editing. Notes are still saved as
  Markdown, so the CLI, agents and exports see the same text.
  - The cursor steps over hidden marks, one visible character per arrow press. Click after a
    bold word and type: the new text is plain (⌘B continues the bold).
  - Backspace and Delete remove the character you see, never half a mark. Deleting a styled
    word's last letter removes its style too, and Backspace at the start of a heading, quote or
    list item turns it back into body text.
  - ⌘B with nothing selected shows nothing until you type.
  - Links show their address, dimmed, while the cursor is inside them, so you can edit it.

## What's new

- **Voice typing (experimental).** Turn it on in Settings → Voice, press **⌃⌥⌘V** anywhere
  and talk: Rallo types into whatever app has focus (a terminal, a browser, your notes), with
  no pasting. Press it again to stop, or pause for 10 s.
  - Three engines: Apple's built-in one (macOS 26), Whisper large-v3 turbo on your Mac (a
    1.6 GB download you start, kept in the shared Hugging Face cache so other Whisper tools can
    use it), or a cloud engine with your own Groq, OpenAI or compatible API key.
  - **Words to recognize** teaches it names like "Rallo" or a teammate's; "um"s and stutters
    ("like like") are dropped.
  - It never presses Return and stays out of password fields. Audio and text are never saved
    or logged.
- **Rallo listens.** While the mic is live the pet cups a paw to its ear, leans in, and nods
  for each phrase it types.

## Fixes and hardening

- Releases no longer carry the `get-task-allow` entitlement, which let other processes of
  yours attach to Rallo (and its new microphone and Accessibility access).
- The Whisper model is checked against its SHA-256 before it is loaded each launch.
- `rallo uninstall` also removes voice API keys from the Keychain.

Cloud engine costs are between you and the provider: Groq's free plan is rate-limited and
costs nothing; Groq's paid plans and OpenAI bill by audio time.

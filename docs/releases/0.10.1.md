## What's new

- **Siri and Shortcuts tell you when a reminder can't alert you.** If notifications for Rallo
  are off (or not allowed yet), "Add Rallo Reminder" still saves the reminder and says it won't
  alert you, and why.
- **More times read the way Siri and Shortcuts write them.** A Date from another Shortcuts
  action ("Oct 9, 2026 at 5:00 PM") and spelled-out numbers ("in two hours", "in forty-five
  minutes", "in an hour", "in half an hour", "tomorrow at nine a.m.") now work in Add Rallo
  Reminder, `rallo remind --at` and the panel's Custom… box.

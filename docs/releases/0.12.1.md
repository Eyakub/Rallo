## What's new

- **Bangla voice typing writes Bangla.** With Whisper or the cloud engine and Language set to
  Bangla, Rallo no longer sends its English word list (Rallo, ClickUp, …) as a hint; that hint
  made Whisper spell Bangla in English letters ("Ami Balaj Kotha Bolchi"). Settings → Voice
  now also says to choose Bangla rather than Automatic, which can mistake Bangla for Hindi.

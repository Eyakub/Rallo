## What's new

- **Reminders you can't miss.** When a reminder comes due, the pet hops to the middle of the
  screen with a bubble: **Done**, **Snooze 10 min** (▾ for the other presets) and **Open**. The
  screen dims a little, and clicks still reach your app. Unanswered, the bubble goes after a
  minute.
  - Rallo asks again every 2 minutes, up to 5 times, until you handle the reminder: Done, a
    snooze, an edit, or Open, from the bubble, the banner, the notes or the CLI.
  - Several at once share one bubble, with "+2 more".
  - An agent waiting a long time gets the same bubble, with **Jump to it** and **Later**.
- **A chime of your own.** Rallo Chime, Bamboo Knock or Gentle Bell (or the system sound, or
  none) plays with the banner, even while Rallo is in front.
- **Settings → Notifications → Alerts:** the pet coming to the centre, the alert sound with a
  preview, repeat until handled (every 1, 2 or 5 minutes, 3, 5 or 10 times), a glow around the
  screen edges (off by default), the same for agent long-waits, and **Open…** for System
  Settings, where "Persistent" keeps Rallo's banners on screen until you click them.
- **Quiet in calls.** While the camera or microphone is in use, the bubble hides the note text
  and repeats are silent. Rallo can't see a Focus on its own, so the pet still comes; a Focus
  mutes the repeat chimes.
- **20-20-20 eye breaks**, off until you turn them on in Settings → Breaks or the menu bar
  menu. Every 20 minutes of screen time, the screens go black for 20 seconds: look at something
  far away.
  - A pill warns 10 seconds ahead, with **Start now**, **+5 min** and **Skip**. Esc skips the
    black screen.
  - **Strict mode** (Allow skipping off): the black screen has no buttons and ignores Esc; it
    ends when its countdown does.
  - A break waits while the camera or microphone is in use or a reminder bubble is up. Five
    minutes away from the keyboard counts as a break, and locking or sleeping the Mac starts a
    fresh 20 minutes.
  - The menu bar menu shows when the next break is, with **Take a Break Now** and **Pause Eye
    Breaks** (30 minutes, 1 hour, or until tomorrow).
  - Settings → Breaks sets how often, how long, how early the warning comes, strict mode, and
    holding during calls.
- **Update badge.** The menu bar paw shows a small arrow when an update is ready, and Rallo now
  checks once a day unless you turned that off.
- **The Notes window's sidebar** has one New Folder button at the bottom (⇧⌘N).

The new alerts are on after the update too: the pet comes to the centre and repeats until you
handle a reminder. Turn either off in Settings → Notifications → Alerts. Your notes keep their
format; Rallo 0.13 can still open them.

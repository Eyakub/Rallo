4 · Decided

Model and CLI

  • Flat folders, one per note; no folder means “Notes”.
  • #tags typed in the text: #bug, #কাজ count; fix #123 and C# don’t.
  • Deleting a folder asks: keep its notes (they go to Notes) or delete them (they go to Deleted).
  • CLI: --folder on note/remind/list/search, move, folder create|rename|delete, folders, tags, list --tag, list --done. An unknown folder name is an error, never a new folder.

Panel and window

  • Shortcut and pet open the panel; expand opens the window; Dock and ⌘-Tab while it’s open.
  • Panel: folder chip (B) plus Move to… on right-click (C).
  • Window: three columns; done notes collapsed at the bottom of a folder; folders alphabetical.
  • “Waiting for you” (agents) stays in the panel only.
  • Move notes by dragging onto a sidebar folder, the Move chip, or Move to….
